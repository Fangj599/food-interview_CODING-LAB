What is your favorite food?

My favorite food is probably spicy noodles. I like noodles because there are so many different ways to make them. You can have them with meat, vegetables, eggs, or different sauces, so they don't always taste the same. I also really like spicy food. I like the feeling of eating something spicy, but I still want to be able to taste the other flavors. If something is so spicy that I can't taste anything else, I don't really enjoy it.

When did you start liking spicy food?

I didn't always like spicy food. When I was younger, I couldn't really handle it. Even food that wasn't very hot felt spicy to me. I think I slowly got used to it because I kept trying different foods. First, I would only eat a little bit of spicy sauce, and then I started eating more. Eventually, I realized that I actually liked the flavor, not just the spice. Now when I look at a menu, I usually notice the spicy options first.

How spicy do you like your food?

I think the best spicy food has a good balance. You should be able to taste the meat, vegetables, sauce, or whatever else is in the dish. I don't really enjoy food where the only thing I can taste is the heat. Sometimes, restaurants ask me how spicy I want something, and I choose a medium or high level depending on the dish. If I've never been there before, I usually start with something less spicy because I don't know how they make it.

What food did you dislike when you were younger but like now?

Spicy food is probably the biggest example. When I was younger, I didn't understand why people wanted to eat something that made their mouths feel hot. I thought it was uncomfortable. As I got older, I started trying different spicy foods and realized that the spice could actually be part of the flavor. I also started liking some vegetables that I didn't like before. I think your taste changes as you get older. Things you didn't like as a kid can become foods you really enjoy later.

What is the most unusual food you have ever tried?

I think the most unusual foods I've tried were things that had a texture I wasn't used to. Sometimes the flavor is actually fine, but the texture makes the food feel strange. I think that's one of the interesting things about trying food from different places. You might see something and think you won't like it, but then you actually enjoy it. I'm usually willing to try something at least once. I don't think you can really know whether you like a food until you actually try it.

What food do you think is overrated?

I think some really expensive restaurant dishes can be overrated. Sometimes you pay a lot of money because the restaurant looks nice, but the actual food isn't that much better than something you could get somewhere cheaper. I don't think price always means quality. For me, I'd rather have a really good meal at a casual restaurant than pay a huge amount just because the restaurant is fancy. I care more about whether I actually enjoy the food.
