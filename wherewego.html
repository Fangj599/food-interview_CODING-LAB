Where We Go

Since you like sharing food with your friends, what food do you usually eat with them?

When I meet my friends, I usually like food that we can share. Hot pot is a good example because everyone can choose what they want and add it to the pot. Korean barbecue is also fun because you can order different meats and share them. Fried chicken is another easy choice. I think food is more enjoyable when you don't have to sit there eating one thing by yourself. When everyone orders different dishes, you can try a little bit of everything. It also gives you something to talk about.

How do you think food brings people together and helps them connect?

I think food gives people a reason to spend time together. When people eat together, they usually talk, share stories, and spend more time with each other. Food is also something that different people can connect over, even if they come from different backgrounds. You can introduce someone to food from your culture, and then they can tell you about something they grew up eating. I think that's one of the best things about food. You don't always need to have the same background to enjoy a meal together.
