How We Feel

What is your favorite place to eat?

I usually prefer casual restaurants. I don't necessarily need a restaurant to look expensive or fancy. I care more about the food and whether I feel comfortable there. I like places where I can sit with friends and talk for a long time without feeling rushed. Small restaurants can actually be some of my favorites because they sometimes have really good food that you wouldn't find in a big chain restaurant. I also like trying new places instead of always going to the same restaurant.

Since you're in the US, what food reminds you of home, like food from your city?

Of course, Chinese food reminds me of home, especially dishes that I used to eat with my family. It's interesting because sometimes it's not even a special dish. It can be something really simple, but the smell or taste reminds you of a certain time. I think food is connected to memories because we eat during so many important moments. Family dinners, holidays, birthdays, and even normal weekends can all involve food. That's probably why certain dishes can make you feel comfortable even when you are somewhere completely different.
